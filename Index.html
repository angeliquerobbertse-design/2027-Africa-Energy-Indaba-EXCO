The main programme of talks and panels with senior speakers.

2–4 MarchExhibition

Companies showing their products and services on stands.

Mostly 4 MarchSpecialist events

Smaller forums on one topic each. Each draws different businesses, so each is a different sales conversation. Pick one on the left.

Across the eventBusiness networking

B2B Connect is the organiser's business-meeting programme (B2B = business to business). Hosted buyers are buyers the organiser invites to meet suppliers.

What this means for us

Each forum is a different client conversation. Match the client to the forum: a cable maker cares about T&D, a solar installer about Solar Africa, a bank about the Investment Forum. Then open with that forum's conversation, not with our titles.
